Daymark is a free and open-source (FOSS) Android application that helps you track the passage of time for your activities, events, and milestones.

Key Features:
- Create activities to track elapsed or remaining time.
- Optional end dates for tracking duration.
- Human-friendly relative time display (e.g., "3 weeks ago").
- Categorise and tag activities for better organisation.
- Privacy-first: All data is stored locally on your device.
- Beautiful Material 3 interface with dynamic theming.
- Secure access with an optional app PIN.
- Data sovereignty: Export your data to JSON or CSV formats.

Daymark is built with a commitment to FOSS values, providing a clean, ad-free experience without any tracking.
