# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11-08-2026

Daymark is a free and open source software (FOSS) Android application.
This privacy policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Summary

- Daymark does not require account sign-in.
- Daymark does not run any analytics or advertising trackers.
- Daymark stores your activity data locally on your device.
- You control export and sharing of your data.

## Data We Process

Daymark stores user-created app content, such as:

- Activity names and notes
- Dates and date ranges
- Categories and tags
- Optional app lock PIN
- App settings and preferences

This data is stored locally using Android app storage (SharedPreferences for settings/state).

## Backup and Restore

Daymark includes optional backup export/import features.

- Export creates a backup file from your local data.
- Import restores data from a file you choose.
- Shared backup files are handled by apps/services you choose to share with.

You are responsible for where you store or share exported backups.

## Network and Third-Party Links

Daymark can open external links when you tap actions such as source code, donation, licence or
privacy policy. These links are opened within the Daymark application.

Daymark itself does not transmit your activity data to any managed server.

## Permissions

Daymark uses only the access needed for core application behaviour, including selecting files for
import and
sharing exported backups.

## Children

Daymark is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically directed to children.
